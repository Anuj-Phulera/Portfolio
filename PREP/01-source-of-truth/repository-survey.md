## The duplication problem

VERDE exists in at least six repositories, RESONATE in two, NIRMAAN in two, SkillVerse in two. A portfolio that lists all of them looks padded; one that lists the best of each looks considered.

**Rule adopted:** one canonical repository per project, named in `../03-projects/fact-sheets/`. Variants are history, not work.

## Consequence for claims

No claim may be made about the unaudited repositories — including any implication that `WEBX-WINNER` is a win — until it is opened and read. The name of a repository is not evidence of anything, which is the same mistake as inferring a school from the word "CBSE".
