## AI-assistance disclosure

Recent repositories show merged branches authored with an AI agent. The portfolio should state, in one calm line, that AI assisted implementation under the author's direction. Reasons:

- It is true and verifiable in the history.
- It removes an attack surface ("did you really write this?") before it is used.
- It is consistent with the package's central rule: no claim without a receipt.

It must not be used as a disclaimer that diminishes the author's decisions. The direction, the constraints, the rejections and the judgment are his.
