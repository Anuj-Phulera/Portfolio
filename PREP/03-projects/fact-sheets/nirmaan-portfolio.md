# NIRMAAN site — fact sheet

**Canonical repo:** `Nirmaan-Portfolio-` · **Surface:** static Next.js marketing site · **Status:** withdrawn from "polished" presentation

## Purpose
To argue, in scenes, why e-waste is a systems problem and why NIRMAN's five-step flow answers it.

## Intended user
Judges, partners, and policy-minded readers who need the problem framed before the product.

## Problem
The site has to make a category feel urgent in thirty seconds and then survive inspection.

## Essential functionality
Boot loader, WebGL hero, pinned manifesto, horizontal debris field, market matrix, five-step flow, UI lightbox, live map, roadmap, team pages.

## Architecture
Next.js static export, GSAP ScrollTrigger, React Three Fiber, Lenis. `/team/anuj` and `/team/aarav` routes.

## Verified contribution
Author of the frontend. Whether the underlying app was solo is a separate, unconfirmed question.

## Collaborators
`/team/aarav` implies a collaborator; confirm scope.

## Evidence of implementation
19 section components, real R3F dependencies, strong section-level content strategy.

## Limitations
Inherited VERDE/RESONATE identity; false 18-second claim; no reduced-motion handling; no accessible fallback for pinned scroll; deployment unverified here.

## Most defensible differentiator
**The argument structure** — Graveyard → Market Matrix → Five Taps. It is the best *thinking* in any of the marketing sites, even where the execution is compromised.

## Portfolio role
Do not present as a finished experience. Present, if at all, as a case study in **rebuilding a project's identity after noticing it had been reused from another one** — which is a more interesting story than the site itself.
