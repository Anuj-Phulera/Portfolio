# RAKSHABANDHAN — fact sheet

**Canonical repo:** `Rakshbandhan` · **Surface:** Next.js narrative site · **Status:** complete narrative, consent unresolved

## Purpose
A personal gift: a ten-chapter site about a sibling relationship across 1,450 km, ending in a rakhi you tie by holding a button.

## Intended user
One person. That is what makes it good.

## Problem
Distance makes an ordinary ritual feel impossible. The site rebuilds the ritual as an interaction.

## Essential functionality
Nine named chapters plus a title route; hand-written Web Audio score; hidden easter eggs; a hold-to-tie interaction; a dossier of seven exhibits; a deliberately incomplete timeline.

## Architecture
Next.js App Router, static export, self-hosted Kalam/Oswald/Space Mono, 245-line Web Audio engine, 75 lines of easter eggs.

## Verified contribution
Sole author. Personal narrative.

## Collaborators
The sister the site is about. **Her consent is a precondition for publication, not a detail.**

## Evidence of implementation
35 commits including the de-fabrication commit, a branded 404, reduced-motion intro skip, and a song credit.

## Limitations
121 MB repository because `.next/cache` is committed; no deployment verified; deeply personal.

## Most defensible differentiator
**The interface admits what it does not know.** `DATE: NOT RECORDED`, `SOMETIME AFTER THAT`, `IDK WHEN` — missing data rendered as missing. This is the most original design decision in the entire body of work, and it was made unprompted.

## Portfolio role
If consent is given: the emotional centrepiece, and the source of the design principle. If not: it stays private, and the principle is applied elsewhere.
