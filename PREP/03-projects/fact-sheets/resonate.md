# RESONATE — fact sheet

**Canonical repo:** `Resonate-Portfolio` · **Surface:** React/Vite artefact inside a pnpm workspace · **Status:** built, never shipped

## Purpose
To argue that recommendation systems should optimise for emotional understanding rather than engagement — "algorithmic empathy."

## Intended user
A general reader drawn in by an unusual narrative; possibly a competition or grant audience.

## Problem
Empathy is the missing variable in algorithmic design, and it is hard to make visible. Shaders and narrative are the instrument.

## Essential functionality
Scroll-driven narrative across dozens of routes, three shader environments, a waitlist terminal, founder pages, manifesto, impact section.

## Architecture
pnpm workspace with `artifacts/resonate` (Vite) and a Next.js migration backup. Three shader pairs, R3F, three, GSAP. Replit-origin configuration.

## Verified contribution
Sole author of the repository.

## Collaborators
None recorded. Founder pages suggest fictional personas, not real collaborators — verify before implying otherwise.

## Evidence of implementation
52 page routes, 12 shader files, real three/R3F dependencies.

## Limitations
No README at root, no live URL, six commits of unresolved deployment configuration. The idea is the strongest of any project; the delivery is the weakest.

## Most defensible differentiator
**The premise.** "Algorithmic empathy" is a genuine thesis, and the shaders give it a face.

## Portfolio role
The unfinished one. Either finish the deployment or present the shaders as source with a still frame. An honest "this never shipped, here is why" is more interesting than another polished lie.
