# Research method

## What this library is not

A mood board. A list of "cool sites." A set of screenshots to imitate.

## What it is

A collection of **design decisions** extracted from work that has survived contact with real audiences, written so that a principle can be reused without the surface being copied.

## Entry format

Each study answers six questions, in this order:

1. **What is it** — one factual sentence.
2. **What it does** — the mechanism, not the vibe.
3. **Why it works** — the human reason, in one paragraph.
4. **What it costs** — the trade-off the author accepted.
5. **What transfers** — a principle stated abstractly enough to apply elsewhere.
6. **What must not transfer** — the specific surface, layout, or identity that would be theft.

## Rules

- Only describe what was actually observed, or what the site itself states. Anything from memory is marked `[recollection]`.
- No adjective without a mechanism behind it. "Beautiful" is not an observation; "the headline is the only element above the fold" is.
- A reference that cannot be fetched is recorded as unavailable rather than described from memory.
- Studies are dated. Sites change.
