# Design principles (provisional)

Derived from the studies, the audits and the subject's own statements. **None of these is approved.** They are candidates, each with the evidence that produced it.

## P1 — Every claim carries a receipt
**From:** the audit pattern (countable claims exact, experiential claims aspirational) and the subject's line *"I'd rather build something that demonstrates its credibility than simply claim that it's credible."*
**Means:** any factual statement on the site can be expanded to show its evidence: a commit, a file, a number, a recording, or the word *unfinished*.
**Kills:** fake terminals, invented metrics, decorative telemetry.

## P2 — Grammar, not template
**From:** The Pudding (a consistent frame, a different form inside it).
**Means:** the experience has a recognisable structure — a way of beginning, cutting, and closing — while each project takes the form it deserves. No shared hero, no shared card, no shared section order.
**Kills:** the fifth skin.

## P3 — Motion must depict change
**From:** Linear (issue moves from Todo to In Progress) and the subject's own misuse of motion as atmosphere.
**Means:** every animation answers "what changed?" If the answer is "nothing, it just looks good," it is decoration and must justify itself or go.

## P4 — Stillness is a material
**From:** anime editing grammar, and the observation that every project in the body of work is loud.
**Means:** at least one deliberate held frame with no motion, no sound, and no scroll prompt. Contrast is what makes movement feel like movement.

## P5 — An interface earns the right to change by explaining itself
**From:** STRATA's design constraints — evidence threshold, rate limit, explanation, pin, never lose your place.
**Means:** if the portfolio adapts at all, it inherits all five constraints, not just the mechanism.

## P6 — Missing data is rendered as missing
**From:** the RAKSHABANDHAN de-fabrication commit (`DATE: NOT RECORDED`).
**Means:** where a fact is unknown, say so in the interface. An unfinished project is labelled unfinished, in the same visual language as a finished one. This is the most original idea in the body of work and it is already proven in public.

## P7 — The site is the work, not an advertisement for the work
**From:** the tension between "take me seriously" and "express what a résumé can't."
**Means:** quality is demonstrated by the artefact — its finish, its honesty, its speed — rather than asserted in copy.

## P8 — Specificity over adjectives
**From:** Craig Mod (sixth printing, printed and bound in Japan) and the audits (exact counts everywhere).
**Means:** name the commit, the number, the failure, the revision. Never "passionate," "innovative," "cutting-edge."

## P9 — Escape hatches are design
**From:** Bruno Simon's "Unstuck" control.
**Means:** every immersive or unusual interaction has a visible, boring way out. The boring path is designed, not tolerated.

## P10 — Finish is the differentiator
**From:** the VERDE final commit (70 dead files pruned, security headers added) and the NIRMAN collapse (mythology replaced by hydration fixes).
**Means:** the last 10% — focus states, empty states, 404, error boundaries, loading behaviour — is where the portfolio proves it is not generated.

## P11 — Cut for rhythm
**From:** anime editing grammar as described in the brief; the observation that documents are cut like documents.
**Means:** decide what the visitor sees, for how long, and what they feel before the next cut. A scroll is a cut, not a scrollbar.
