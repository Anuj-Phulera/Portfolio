# Three directions — framing

**Status:** explorations. None is approved. No direction may be implemented until the subject chooses or recombines them.

## The test every direction must pass

1. **The north-star test.** After experiencing it, can a visitor name one thing this person might build next?
2. **The removal test.** With every anime reference removed, does it still have an identity?
3. **The receipt test.** Does it make claims the subject can defend in a room of experts?
4. **The fifth-skin test.** Is it structurally unlike VERDE, NIRMAN, RESONATE and RAKSHABANDHAN?
5. **The boring-path test.** Can a tired recruiter on a five-year-old phone get the facts in ninety seconds?
6. **The build-truth test.** Can this be built and finished by one person, alone, to the standard it promises?

## Constraints that apply to all three

- No fabricated telemetry, no fake boot checks, no decorative HUD.
- No autoplay audio. Sound only after an explicit gesture.
- Reduced motion is a designed path, not a global off-switch.
- Every project page states attribution and status.
- Content exists for eight projects; the design must survive a project being withdrawn (RAKSHABANDHAN pending consent, NIRMAAN withdrawn).
- The portfolio itself is the ninth project and must be inspectable as one.
