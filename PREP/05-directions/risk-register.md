# Risk register — directions

Risks are ranked by **likelihood × damage**, not by how interesting they are to discuss.

| # | Risk | Direction | Likelihood | Damage | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | The bench (C) takes longer than available and ships half-built | C | High | High | Build one instrument to full finish before building four. If only one is excellent, the direction still works. |
| R2 | The rooms (B) read as pretentious mood boards | B | Medium | High | Five texts must be edited like essays; each room must contain a checkable fact. |
| R3 | The ledger (A) reads as a documentation site with no pulse | A | High | Medium | One moment of craft per entry — a transition, a stamp, a receipt — and a single designed surprise. |
| R4 | Hybridisation produces mush | all | Medium | High | If recombining, one direction owns composition; the others contribute rules only. |
| R5 | Accessibility is retrofitted | all | High | High | Checkers run from the first commit; reduced-motion path designed with the main path. |
| R6 | Mobile becomes a degraded desktop | B, C | High | High | Design the 390 px layout first for the chosen direction. |
| R7 | The site becomes the fifth skin | all | Medium | High | The fifth-skin test is a gate, not a review note. |
| R8 | Content is not ready (consent, captures, corrected copy) | all | Medium | High | `../09-assets/needed.md` is a blocking list, not a wish list. |
| R9 | Claims creep back in during copywriting | all | Medium | High | Ledger validator runs in CI; copy is written from `safe_wording` only. |
| R10 | Performance collapses under effects | B, C | High | Medium | A hard budget per route; one live canvas at a time; measure before adding. |
| R11 | The subject loses interest during a long build | all | Medium | High | Ship a vertical slice in weeks, not a whole experience in months. |
| R12 | The result is impressive but says nothing about the person | all | Medium | Fatal to the brief | Every direction must carry at least three irreplaceable personal facts. |

## The two risks that actually decide the project

**R1 and R12.** Everything else is recoverable. A half-built bench fails the "finish is the differentiator" principle; a technically perfect site that could belong to anyone fails the entire brief.

## Kill criteria

The direction must be abandoned or re-scoped if, at the vertical-slice review:

- a visitor cannot state one thing the subject might build next;
- the fastest route through the experience takes more than 90 seconds to reach the work;
- the reduced-motion version loses content rather than motion;
- the build is behind by more than one week against the roadmap in `../10-blueprint/roadmap.md`.
