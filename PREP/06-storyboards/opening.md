# Openings — three candidates, 30 seconds each

All three assume a cold visitor, a mid-range phone, and no sound. Each must work with motion disabled.

## A. The Ledger — "the record is already open"

| Time | Shot | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0 | Paper, full viewport | No animation. A single line of type, upper left. |
| 1.2 | Same | A date and a status fade in beneath it: `ENTRY 009 · 21 OCT 2026 · IN PROGRESS`. |
| 2.6 | Same | The index begins below the fold; four of eight entries carry stamps. |
| 6.0 | Same | A marginal note appears beside entry one: `This portfolio is the ninth project.` |
| 10–30 | Reader scrolls | Nothing chases them. The page is complete from the first second. |

**Feeling:** composure, curiosity, slight intrigue at the word *unfinished*.
**Cost:** no spectacle. Retention depends entirely on the typography being excellent.

## B. Threshold — "the door is already ajar"

| Time | Shot | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0 | Near-black | A 2 px vertical line of amber light, centre. |
| 0.6–1.8 | Same | The line widens into a doorway, 1.2 s, long ease. |
| 1.8–3.0 | Same | Nothing moves. A deliberate hold. |
| 3.0 | Same | `ANUJ PHULERA` appears low, small. |
| 4.2 | Same | `Five rooms. Some of them are unfinished.` |
| 6.0 | Same | `Contents` appears as a real link, not hidden. |
| 8.0 | First scroll | Light grows; the corridor wall passes; the Desk resolves at ~11 s. |

**Feeling:** anticipation, intimacy, the sense of being let in.
**Cost:** 8 seconds before any content; requires an escape hatch and a fast path.

## C. Instrument — "something is already running"

| Time | Shot | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0 | Off-white bench | One instrument idles: three layouts cycling on its own, slowly. |
| 1.0 | Same | Label above: `BENCH 01 · SEVEN SYSTEMS · FOUR YOU CAN TOUCH`. |
| 3.0 | Same | Pointer moves; the instrument reacts. A tab appears: `What am I looking at?` |
| 5.0 | Same | The visitor drags a control; the layout changes; a readout reports the read. |
| 8.0 | Same | `PIN THIS MODE` becomes visible. |
| 12–30 | Reader explores | Each further instrument is already doing something, so the room feels inhabited. |

**Feeling:** curiosity, competence, the urge to touch.
**Cost:** the first instrument must be genuinely good; if it is a toy, the whole direction dies in five seconds.

## What all three refuse

- No fake loading percentage.
- No "scroll to explore" as the only instruction.
- No sound before a gesture.
- No animation that delays access to the first real sentence.

## The opening test

Show the first 6 seconds to someone who has never heard of the subject, with sound off, then close the tab. Ask: *what do you think this person does?* If the answer is "makes websites," the opening failed. The target answer is something specific to the person — "builds systems that watch how you read," or "documents his own work unusually honestly."
