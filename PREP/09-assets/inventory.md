## Does not exist and must be created

- **A portrait of the subject.** No usable photograph was found in the repositories. A portfolio about a person needs one real image of that person, or a deliberate decision to have none.
- **A written account of each project's failure** — 150–300 words each, in the subject's own voice.
- **A 40-second recording of the VERDE dashboard** working, or a labelled replay fixture.
- **Corrected project copy** for every claim marked `X` in the ledger.
- **A decision on the survey's public representation** (aggregate charts only).
- **Consent** for RAKSHANDHAN.

## The rule

An asset is not “available” because it exists in a repository. It is available when its **rights, accuracy and consent** are all recorded.
