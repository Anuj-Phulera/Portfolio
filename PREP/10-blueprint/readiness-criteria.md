# Readiness criteria

The build may begin only when every item below is true. These are gates, not suggestions.

## Creative

- [ ] One direction is chosen, recorded in `decision-log.md`, with a one-paragraph rationale in the subject's own words.
- [ ] The recombination rule is stated (which direction owns composition; which only contributes rules).
- [ ] The opening, one middle sequence and the ending are storyboarded at the chosen direction's fidelity.
- [ ] The subject has stated what would make him abandon the direction.

## Content

- [ ] All blocking items N1–N6 in `../09-assets/needed.md` are answered.
- [ ] Every `X` claim has corrected public copy, or the project is withdrawn.
- [ ] Attribution sentences are approved by the people they name.
- [ ] Consent for RAKSHANDHAN is recorded, or the project is excluded entirely.
- [ ] The portrait decision is made: a real photograph, or a deliberate absence with a stated reason.

## Technical

- [ ] Stack chosen and recorded, with the reason and the rejected alternative.
- [ ] The motion baseline from the lab is adopted or explicitly rejected.
- [ ] The performance budget is accepted as a constraint on design, not a post-launch metric.
- [ ] A browser and device are available for testing (this environment has neither).

## Quality

- [ ] `tools/claims/validate.mjs`, `tools/quality/audit.mjs`, `tools/design/contrast.mjs` and the lab tests all pass.
- [ ] The accessibility procedure has an owner and a scheduled first pass.
- [ ] A rollback path exists before the first deploy.

## The one criterion that overrides the rest

**The subject can explain, in one sentence and without notes, what the site is arguing.** If that sentence does not exist, no amount of readiness in the list will save the build.
